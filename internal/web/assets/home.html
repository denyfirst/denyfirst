02 / The standard we build to

Less blind trust.
More understanding.

01

Evidence first.

Show what was observed, cite the rule behind a conclusion, and say plainly what was not measured.

02

Control stays with you.

Built to be run on your own machine. What is kept and what is contacted are stated, and nothing is collected by us.

03

Keep the scope clear.

Purposeful instruments with explicit boundaries: a check reads what a server already serves, and does nothing more.

We are an independent team building security and privacy tools. denyfirst is the home for that work: the products, the reasoning behind them, and the responsibility for both. Everything we ship is open source, with no third-party dependencies, so it can be checked rather than trusted.
